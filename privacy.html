Privacy policy

Gmail Reader is a personal tool used only by the owner of this GitHub account, on their own Gmail account.

  • It requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Email data is read on the owner's own computer and is not sent to, stored by, or shared with this website or any other third party by this tool.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • No other users' data is collected.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
